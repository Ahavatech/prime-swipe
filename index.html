How to play

Swipe the board, or use the arrow keys or W A S D. Every tile slides as far as it can.

When two touching tiles add up to a prime, they merge into that prime. A 3 next to an 8 becomes an 11, because 3 + 8 = 11.

Two odd tiles add up to an even number, and so do two even tiles, so a useful merge needs one of each. Even tiles are your fuel. The one exception is 1 + 1 = 2, which merges even though 1 is not prime.

  • Even tile
  • Odd tile, darker as it grows

Every swipe that moves something adds one new tile. A tile built by a merge waits until the next swipe to merge again.

Build a 101 to reach the goal, then keep going. The run ends when the board is full and no neighbors add up to a prime.

Spawn weights and parity assist

Starting weights: 1 (20), 2 (25), 3 (10), 4 (20), 5 (10), 6 (15). At a board tile of 23, 8, 10, and 12 unlock; at 53, 14 and 16 unlock. Each unlocked tile has weight 5.

If there are fewer even tiles than odd tiles, even spawns gain 20 percentage points and odd spawns lose 20. Tile 1 counts as odd. Active weights are normalized; within each parity, relative weights stay the same.
