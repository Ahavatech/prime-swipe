How to play

Slide in any direction. Tiles pack toward that edge, then neighboring tiles merge only when their sum is prime.

A new tile appears after every move that changes the board. A merged tile cannot merge again until your next swipe.

1 is not prime, but 1 + 1 = 2 is a legal merge.

Spawn weights and parity assist

Starting weights: 1 (20), 2 (25), 3 (10), 4 (20), 5 (10), 6 (15). At a board tile of 23, 8, 10, and 12 unlock; at 53, 14 and 16 unlock. Each unlocked tile has weight 5.

If there are fewer even tiles than odd tiles, even spawns gain 20 percentage points and odd spawns lose 20. Tile 1 counts as odd. Active weights are normalized; within each parity, relative weights stay the same.
